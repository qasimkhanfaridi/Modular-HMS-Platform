# 07 — Sales Guide

How to sell PulseCore HMS from **small heart clinic** to **RIC enterprise**.

---

## Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)

> *"PulseCore is a modular hospital system — start with patient registration and OPD for your heart clinic at PKR 25,000/month. Add pharmacy, lab, emergency, or ICU modules whenever you're ready. Same system powers small clinics today and will power RIC-scale hospitals tomorrow — no migration, no new software."*

## Demo Script — Heart Clinic (15 min)

1. **Registration** (2 min) — New patient, CNIC scan, MR number, photo
2. **Appointment** (1 min) — Book tomorrow, SMS confirmation
3. **OPD Consult** (5 min) — Cardiac history, ECG entry, risk score, prescription
4. **Pharmacy** (2 min) — E-Rx appears, dispense, stock update
5. **Reports** (2 min) — Today's OPD count, patient list export
6. **Upgrade tease** (3 min) — "When you add echo lab, enable M09 — same login, new menu"

**Demo environment:** Pre-loaded with 50 fake patients, cardiology templates.

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Too expensive for my clinic" | PKG-A = PKR 25K/month. Less than one patient consultation fee per day. |
| "We use paper fine" | RIC started digital OPD and now handles 3000 patients/day. Start small. |
| "We tried HMS before, failed" | Modular — start with 5 features, not 500. 2-week install. |
| "What if we outgrow it?" | Add modules. Clinic → hospital → institute. Same system. |
| "We're not a heart clinic" | Disable cardiac modules. General hospital preset available. |
| "Internet is unreliable" | Offline mode for registration and OPD. Sync when connected. |
| "Who maintains it?" | Annual support contract. Local help desk. Remote support included. |

## Pilot Programme (First 5 Clinics)

Offer **50% discount Year 1** for first 5 pilot clinics in exchange for:
- Case study permission
- Monthly feedback call
- Reference for future sales
- Logo on website

**Pilot criteria:**
- Heart-related clinic in Rawalpindi/Islamabad/Lahore
- Willing to go live within 30 days
- Minimum 30 patients/day
- 1 doctor champion on site

## RIC Sales Strategy (Phase 5)

### Do NOT pitch RIC first
RIC is the **anchor**, not the first customer.

### When to approach RIC
- After 3+ clinics live (Phase 1 complete)
- After 1 hospital live (Phase 3 complete)
- During HMIS tender window (2026 tender active)

### RIC Pitch Angle
*"Our OPD and Pharmacy modules are already running at [X] clinics and [Y] hospital, processing [Z] patients/day. "
